The process

Four stages, one clean loop

Electrolysis

An electric current splits water (H₂O) into hydrogen and oxygen.

Compression & storage

Hydrogen is compressed and held in safe, certified tanks.

Fuel cell conversion

Hydrogen and oxygen react to release electricity and water.

Smart control

Monitoring keeps every stage efficient and safe.

Fuel cells

Electricity without combustion

A fuel cell converts chemical energy straight into electricity. There is no burning, so there are no harmful exhaust gases.

  • Direct, efficient conversion
  • Silent operation
  • Water is the only by-product
